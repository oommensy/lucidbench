# Collaboration Strategy

## First target: Ken Paller / Cognitive Neuroscience Laboratory, Northwestern University

Why:

- Active lucid-dream program.
- Real-time communication with dreamers during REM.
- 2026 work on dream perception and dream engineering.
- Natural path from computational report analysis to experimentally validated dream markers.

Potential contribution:

> Test whether semantic markers of awareness, agency, control, and waking-memory access derived from large-scale public dream reports generalize to laboratory-verified lucid-dream reports and real-time REM reports.

## Other strong targets

- Martin Dresler / Donders Institute & Radboud University — lucid dreaming, sleep neurophysiology, EEG.
- Daniel Erlacher / University of Bern — lucid-dream motor learning and experimental dream research.
- Michelle Carr / Center for Advanced Research in Sleep Medicine, Université de Montréal — dream engineering, EEG, text analysis.
- Pattie Maes / MIT Media Lab — sleep and dream engineering / human-computer interaction.

## Outreach rule

Do **not** send a generic “I am interested in lucid dreaming” email.

Contact researchers after the repo has:

1. a working baseline;
2. one author-disjoint result;
3. one vocabulary-ablation result;
4. a short error analysis;
5. one figure showing something scientifically interesting.

The outreach then becomes a concrete research proposition rather than a request for mentorship.
