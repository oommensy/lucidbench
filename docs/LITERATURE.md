## Literature questions to track

- Operational definitions of lucidity vs control.
- Validated dream-content coding systems.
- Real-time signaling during REM sleep.
- Reliability of retrospective dream reports.
- Computational text analysis of dream narratives.
- Cross-dreamer generalization and repeated-measures statistics.
